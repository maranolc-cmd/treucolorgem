Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

LiveProof ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use the LiveProof platform at liveproof.ai.

1. Data We Collect

When you register and use LiveProof, we collect:

  • Account data: name, email address, date of birth, phone number, country of residence, and selling platform
  • Certification photos: listing photos and live reference photos you submit for AI analysis
  • Device data: a non-personally-identifiable device fingerprint used to prevent abuse
  • Usage data: certification history, scores, and buyer verification events
  • Payment data: processed by Stripe; we do not store card details

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the certification service and generate your certificates
  • To allow buyers to verify certificates via QR code (public certificate page shows item name, score, and listing photo)
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To prevent fraud and abuse of the platform
  • To improve our AI analysis accuracy

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the following service providers:

  • Anthropic: listing and live photos are sent to Claude AI for analysis. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to this data.
  • Google Firebase: authentication, database, and file storage
  • Stripe: payment processing
  • Vercel: hosting infrastructure

Your listing photo and certification details are made publicly accessible via the QR verification page so buyers can verify your certificates.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Certification records are retained indefinitely to allow buyers to verify past certificates. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@liveproof.ai.

6. Cookies

LiveProof uses only functional cookies necessary for authentication and session management. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), Firebase security rules, and tokenized authentication to protect your data.

8. Children

LiveProof is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of significant changes by email.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at hello@liveproof.ai.
